Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Important: GharRent Pakistan is a rental-property discovery and listing platform. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website. It is written for a website that may use analytics, cookies and third-party advertising services. It does not create a guarantee that every third-party service is enabled at all times.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive information such as search terms, approximate location information, device and browser information, pages viewed, referral information, and information you voluntarily enter into a property listing or contact form.

Property information submitted through the current browser-only listing feature may be stored locally in your browser. Do not enter passwords, payment-card information, identity-document numbers or other sensitive information into public listing fields.

2. How we use information

  • To provide property search and listing features.
  • To improve website performance, usability and content.
  • To understand aggregate traffic and website usage.
  • To detect abuse, spam, fraud or security problems.
  • To respond to enquiries, reports or support requests.
  • To operate advertising and measurement services where enabled.

3. Property listings and public information

Information that a property owner chooses to publish may be visible to other website visitors. Before publishing a listing, users should avoid unnecessary personal information and should only provide contact information they are comfortable making available for rental enquiries.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

GharRent may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for essential functionality, preferences, security, analytics and advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect website functionality.

5. Advertising and Google services

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve or measure ads and may process information according to their own policies. Where required, GharRent will provide appropriate consent choices and disclosures before using non-essential cookies or personalised advertising.

Users can review Google's information about advertising and privacy through Google's own privacy and advertising controls.

6. Third-party services and links

The website may link to or use third-party services such as hosting, analytics, fonts, maps or advertising providers. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. GharRent does not control how an independent third party processes information once you leave our website or interact directly with that provider.

7. Data retention and security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the service to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, legal obligations, dispute handling, security and legitimate business needs.

8. Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional information. You can also clear local browser storage and manage cookies through your browser. If you have submitted information to GharRent and need it corrected or removed, contact us using the contact method published on the website.

9. Children's privacy

GharRent is intended for people who are legally able to use a rental marketplace. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us so appropriate action can be considered.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, technology, advertising arrangements or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or concerns about a listing, please use the contact/support option on GharRent Pakistan.
